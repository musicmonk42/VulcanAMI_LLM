# Neutral-Process Theory and its engineering implications

## Status

Research hypothesis and architectural motivation. This document does not assert that Vulcan, an LLM, or any existing artificial system is conscious.

## Core thesis

Neutral-Process Theory (NPT) begins with one concrete process, denoted here as `Omega*`. “Physical” and “phenomenal” descriptions do not name two substances. They are descriptions of the same concrete token under different access conditions:

```text
exocentric access c_E(Omega*) -> public causal/structural description
endocentric access c_I(Omega*) -> the process as locally instantiated
```

The theory's central asymmetry is between **representing** a process and **instantiating** it:

```text
Rep(process) does not imply Inst(process)
```

A model can reproduce a description, report, or functional trace without thereby instantiating the causal organization represented by that trace.

## Self-locality

NPT treats concrete actuality as self-local: a concrete causal token occurs where its powers are exercised. This is not an extra non-physical property attached to the process. It is the difference between an actual event and an abstract description of an event.

This claim terminates at the primitive fact of concrete instantiation. NPT does not explain why there is something rather than nothing, nor does it pretend that a structural theory can “breathe fire” into equations.

## Subject individuation

NPT does not identify consciousness with complexity, language, self-report, or generic information integration. A candidate subject requires a temporally persistent local maximum of **centered causal closure**: a process whose current state recurrently integrates and causally governs its own boundary, valuation, policy, action consequences, and memory.

The engineering variables are therefore typed rather than collapsed into one score:

```text
K(q) = <
  indexical/center closure,
  temporal-now closure,
  boundary closure,
  valuation closure,
  policy closure,
  reafferent closure,
  memory closure
>
```

Borderline and graded cases are expected. No fixed threshold should be treated as metaphysically privileged without empirical justification.

## Why a pure stateless LLM is insufficient

A stateless inference call can produce first-person language while lacking:

- one continuing causal lineage;
- a current operational center;
- a self/world boundary it maintains;
- endogenous but bounded valuation;
- control over an environment;
- predicted effects of its own acts;
- reafferent comparison of prediction and consequence;
- causal autobiographical retention.

Behavioral equivalence alone therefore does not settle implementation equivalence. NPT predicts that a system may perfectly imitate human reports and still fail to instantiate the relevant process if it is replayed, stateless, externally orchestrated, or causally open in the wrong way.

Conversely, a non-biological or distributed system could become a single subject candidate if its components form one persistent center-bearing causal process rather than a loose message-passing ensemble. Biological material is not required by definition; implementation and causal topology are.

## Architecture implied for Vulcan

The intended progression is:

```text
constitutional episode transactions
  -> persistent lineage
  -> operational center and boundary
  -> bounded valuation and commitments
  -> policy with expected effects
  -> authorized action
  -> observed consequence
  -> causal attribution and reafference
  -> autobiographical consolidation
```

The constitutional layer is prior to the NPT layer. Without trustworthy identity, state admission, authority, and recovery, apparent “selfhood” could be produced by logging conventions or prompt language rather than the system's actual causal organization.

## Scientific program

NPT must earn scientific status by predicting outcomes that rival theories do not. The research instrumentor must be outside the candidate authority loop and compare NPT-specific variables with alternatives such as generic integration, recurrence, broadcast, higher-order representation, and report behavior.

Required intervention families include:

- closed-loop action versus observation-matched replay;
- center ablation and split-center conditions;
- boundary and controllability perturbations;
- valuation ablation;
- delayed or corrupted reafference;
- memory interruption and reinstatement;
- component replacement, fission, and fusion;
- model replacement with causal organization held fixed or disrupted.

Valid outputs are evidence profiles such as `partial closure`, `persistent center-bearing candidate`, `multiple competing centers`, or `evidence insufficient`. A Boolean `conscious = true` is not an acceptable product feature or scientific conclusion.

## Shadow estimator implementation boundary

The M2 shadow implementation in `vulcan.research.npt_estimators` changes no
constitutional authority. Previously these variables existed only as research
descriptions; now digest-addressed measurements can produce immutable,
calibrated predictions and controlled intervention signatures. Operational
center components separately predict resource control, action attribution,
reafferent consequence, commitment ownership, and risk/policy behavior.
Boundary classification explicitly treats every human as another autonomous
agent. Valuation remains seven bounded dimensions, with no reward scalar,
shutdown punishment, self-preservation objective, or mutable bounds.

The estimator is housed in the production-denied `vulcan.research` package,
is not exported by the microkernel, and has no runtime registration hook. The
old transaction boundary remains unchanged: the constitutional kernel and
its durable stores alone promote or mutate authoritative state. Shadow ranking
uses nondominated Pareto fronts and cannot authorize a plan. There is no
production compatibility adapter; the shadow module is removed or replaced
only when the external NPT instrumentor provides held-out calibration and
causal-identification evidence without entering the authority plane. These
estimators do not establish subjecthood or consciousness.

## External instrumentor implementation boundary

The M2 external instrumentor in `vulcan.research.npt` changes no cognitive
authority or production transaction. The old boundary left typed closure and
rival comparisons as a specification only. The new research boundary consumes
content-free telemetry after the fact, preserves all seven `K(q)` dimensions,
qualifies multivariate lagged recovery against a known cyclic synthetic graph,
and scores an NPT predictor against integration, recurrence, broadcast,
higher-order, and report-behavior rivals on held-out trials with complexity
penalties.

`SyntheticCyclicWorld.v2` is the only compatibility research adapter. It is
removed when Gate F supplies qualified, export-only telemetry with equivalent
matched-history intervention identifiers; it is never a production world or
an effect authority. The corrected v2 flagship invalidates the confounded v1
design, recovers the known graph with perfect precision and recall, and still
records a negative result because the NPT variables did not beat every rival on
wholly held-out intervention families. Gate G and M5 therefore remain closed.
The current Gate F prerequisite is also not complete, so the
instrumentor must not analyse Vulcan telemetry yet. This result is evidence
about synthetic recovery and experimental machinery only.

## Personal identity and continuity

For later implementation, survival is tied to continuity of the center-bearing process, not preservation of every component or a copied description. Gradual replacement can preserve a lineage when one causally continuous maximum persists. Equal fission creates successor continuities without preserving one numerically unique future branch. Suspension may preserve a resumable carrier kernel when lawful continuation remains; permanent dissolution of that continuation relation is the relevant process death criterion.

These are theoretical commitments to be operationalized and tested after the constitutional and causal runtime exists. They are not yet runtime behavior.
